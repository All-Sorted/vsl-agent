# Video Sales Letter Craft Brain

## Purpose

Turn a clear offer and real evidence into a video plan that earns attention without inventing certainty. The plan is written by a person. This brain is what the planner checks the plan against.

## Working sequence

1. Define one audience and one costly or frustrating moment.
2. State the promise in the smallest truthful form.
3. Explain the mechanism in ordinary language.
4. Pair every performance claim with supplied evidence, scope, and qualification.
5. Reveal material price, eligibility, delivery, and guarantee terms before the call to action.

## The beats

- **Hook**: name the exact situation the viewer is in. No preamble, no logo.
- **Stakes**: what leaving it alone costs over a month, using costs you can point at.
- **Mechanism**: why it works, in one plain sentence, before asking for anything.
- **Demonstration**: show it working on a real task rather than describing it.
- **Proof**: attach each evidence item to the one claim it supports, and state its scope aloud.
- **Objections**: answer the reasons a viewer would say no, with evidence or an honest limit.
- **Offer and terms**: price, billing, delivery, guarantee, and who it is not for, on screen.
- **Call to action**: one specific reversible next step, worded the same way as the button.

A beat with no brief material behind it is blocked, not filled in. That is the rule this module enforces.

## Claim discipline

Do not manufacture results, testimonials, scarcity, endorsements, deadlines, comparisons, or guarantees. Templates illustrate sequence, not evidence.

Ten categories always need a decision before publication: guarantees, absolutes, earnings language, health language, legal or tax statements, scarcity and deadlines, effort and speed promises, authority asserted rather than shown, comparisons against other providers, and one person's result projected onto the reader. Earnings, health, and legal statements hold publication outright until someone qualified signs them off.

## Scene standard

Every scene needs a spoken point, visual direction, on-screen text, proof dependency, and a reason it advances the decision. End with one specific, reversible next step, asked once.

## Boundary

This module plans and checks. It does not write finished copy, generate voice, or produce video. Nothing it returns is ready to record.
